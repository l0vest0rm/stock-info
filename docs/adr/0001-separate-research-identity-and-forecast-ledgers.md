# Separate research identity and forecast ledgers

Investment research uses a stable operating-company identity plus distinct listed securities, and stores source forecasts, local-model synthesis drafts, deterministic consolidations, analyst scenarios and actual calibrations in separate ledgers. This avoids using a ticker as a company ID or promoting expiring report caches and incomplete samples into company facts or market consensus; the extra joins and explicit unresolved states are accepted in exchange for auditable A/H/ADR research and historical calibration.
