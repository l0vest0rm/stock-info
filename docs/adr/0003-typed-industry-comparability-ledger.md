# Typed industry comparability ledger

Industry research profiles, company track exposures and peer comparison sets are stored as versioned relational records with evidence references attached to each material field group. They are not saved as a generic dossier JSON payload.

The model accepts more tables and more explicit write forms in exchange for preserving the meaning of an attributable business share, an excluded peer, and a cross-market adjustment. This prevents a page label or unqualified peer list from being reused as a financial or valuation comparison conclusion, while retaining immutable history as track boundaries and accounting bases change.
